PHAN II: TONG QUAN VAN DE / YEU CAU (OVERVIEW & CONTEXT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Thuc trang nghiep vu kiem thu:
   Trong quy trinh kiem thu an toan chuc nang o to (ISO 26262 ASIL D) doi voi he truyen dong dien
   (FMCU - Front Motor Control Unit, CVC - Central Vehicle Controller, ban tin CVC_FMCU_Ctrl ID 0x0AB),
   ky su kiem thu can chu dong lam sai lech co che bao ve E2E de kiem tra phan ung an toan cua ECU:
   - Tiem loi CRC doc lap: Ghim co dinh gia tri CRC = 0x00 (hoac sai lech) trong khi Alive van tang deu
     de xac minh ECU bat dung ma loi E2E_E_WRONG_CRC ma khong bi nham voi loi Alive.
   - Tiem loi Alive doc lap: Ghim co dinh gia tri Alive = 0 (Frozen / Stuck counter) trong khi CRC
     van duoc tinh khop toan hoc theo gia tri Alive ket de xac minh ECU bat dung loi E2E_E_REPEATED.
   - Tiem loi kep: Ghim ca CRC va Alive co dinh.

2. Hien tuong loi phat hien:
   - Tren Simulate: Khi nguoi dung tick Override cho CRC = 0, byte CRC khong giu nguyen ma bi
     E2eProtector tinh lai va ghi de len byte CRC, trong khi Alive van nhay theo chu ky.
   - Tren xe that: Khi ban Cyclically ghi de ban tin CVC_FMCU_Ctrl song song voi CVC that tren bus,
     xung dot CAN ID (ID Collision) sinh ra bao Error Frame lam Bus Load vot 100%, dan den lag UI CAN;
     dong thoi ECU xe phat hien vi pham E2E vuot nguong debounce (3 chu ky = 300ms) nen kich hoat
     Safe Torque Off (STO) va mo ro-le pin cao ap (High Voltage Contactor), dua xe vao Limp-Home.
