1. MO TA VAN DE & YEU CAU CUA NGUOI DUNG:
   - Van de 1 (Ban frame som): Khi nguoi dung tich chon checkbox Ghi de (Override) trong bang THIET LAP GIA TRI TIN HIEU (Signal Value Setup), he thong lap tuc can thiep va phat frame sua doi ra bus CH2 lien tuc theo chu ky xe that, hoan toan bo qua bang THAO TAC NHANH (Quick Action).
   - Van de 2 (Treo / Treo lap khi Gui mot lan): Nut GUI MOT LAN (One-Shot) khi bam bi treo trong Task.Delay(-1), khoa luoi SignalValueGrid, va khong hoan thanh duoc 1 lan ban duy nhat.
   - Van de 3 (Thieu hieu ung thi giac): Cac nut trong bang Quick Action (GUI MOT LAN, GUI TAT CA MOT LAN, DUNG TAT CA TUAN HOAN, XOA BO DEM, DUNG KHAN CAP) thieu trigger IsPressed (khi nhan chuot khong lun xuong / khong doi mau nhan), va trang thai IsEnabled=False khong hien thi ro rang lam nguoi dung khong phan biet duoc nut co bam duoc hay khong.

2. NGUYEN NHAN GOC RE (ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS):
   - Root Cause 1: Trong SIMULATE.xaml.cs (su kien OnSignalPropertyChanged va SyncOverridesFromUi), khi co bat ky signal nao co Override = true, code tu dong gan:
       selectedTx.Mode = GatewayMode.Inject;
       _mitmEngine.UpdateTxList(_txMessages);
     Dieu nay lam cho GatewayLoop trong MitmEngine.cs ngay lap tuc bat moi frame CH1 trung ID, dong goi signal ghi de va phat ra CH2 lien tuc ma khong can lenh tu Quick Action.
   - Root Cause 2: Trong ExecuteSingleStepAsync, nhanh One-Shot co doan code:
       if (typeName == One-Shot) {
           SignalValueGrid.IsEnabled = false;
           await Task.Delay(-1, token);
           return;
       }
     Doan code nay khien thao tac One-Shot bi bien thanh infinite wait, lam do UI grid va chi dung khi bi cancel ngoai le.
   - Root Cause 3: ControlTemplate cua HeaderButtonStyle, GreenButtonStyle, RedButtonStyle, BlueButtonStyle, SendOnceButtonStyle, ActionButtonStyle, ToolbarButtonStyle trong SIMULATE.xaml chi co trigger IsMouseOver. Hoan toan thieu:
       + Trigger Property=IsPressed Value=True
       + Trigger Property=IsEnabled Value=False
     Khi nut bi vo hieu hoa hoac khi duoc click, giao dien giu nguyen khong phan hoi.
