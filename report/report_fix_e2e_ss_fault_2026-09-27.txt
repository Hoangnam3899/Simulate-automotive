2. MO TA CHI TIET MUC DICH VA LOGIC THAY DOI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Van de thuc te tren xe:
  Khi cam CAN qua Breakout Box (BOB) ngat doi duong CAN (Can so -> CH2 RX, CH1 TX -> Xe/CVC_S),
  tin hieu SS_Fault (Message SS_DriveReq_0x108) khi inject gia tri 1 (Shifter failure) bi chap
  chon, hien thi dang song vuong 50/50 tren TSMaster giua normal (0) va Shifter failure (1).
- Nguyen nhan goc re:
  + Nguyen nhan 1 (Double Transmission): Luong Gateway (RunReceiveLoopAsync) nhan frame tu CH2,
    ghi de tin hieu va ban ngay sang CH1. Dong thoi, luong TransmitScheduledFrameAsync do co rule
    SendType = Cyclic nen cung ban chu ky 50ms sang CH1. Hai luong cung ban dan den 100 frame/giay,
    lech pha du lieu.
  + Nguyen nhan 2 (Alive Counter de-sync): Luong TransmitScheduledFrameAsync tu sinh bo dem counter
    rieng biet tu 0..14 doc lap voi Can so that, khien CVC nhan 2 chuoi counter nhay coc, bao loi
    Sequence Error va drop frame hoac luan phien fallback ve normal.
- Giai phap ky thuat:
  + Trong E2eProtector: Bo sung phuong thuc ApplyPreservingCounter. Phuong thuc nay bao luu 100%
    4-bit Alive Counter goc cua Can so phan cung, sau do tinh toan lai Checksum CRC8 theo chuan
    SAE J1850 (Data ID = 0x82) tren payload da inject tin hieu moi.
  + Trong SimulationEngine:
    * Them _lastRxTimestamps theo doi live traffic theo CAN ID tu cong RX.
    * Trong RunReceiveLoopAsync: Khi chuyen tiep tu RX sang TX voi rule Inject/PassThrough, goi
      ApplyE2eProtection(..., preserveLiveCounter: true) de ke thua nguyen ven Alive Counter.
    * Trong TransmitScheduledFrameAsync: Kiem tra neu rule la Cyclic va dang co live traffic tu RX
      trong vong 500ms, ham se tu dong bo qua (skip), loai bo hoan toan hien tuong Double Transmission.
      Neu xe tat may hoac ngat can so (> 500ms khong co Rx), scheduler se tu dong tiep quan ban chu ky.
  + Bo sung unit test ApplyPreservingCounter_preserves_existing_counter_and_calculates_checksum
    dat 100% do phu cho ham moi.
  + Cap nhat LanguageServiceTests dam bao tinh co lap va doc lap moi truong test.
