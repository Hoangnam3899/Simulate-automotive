I. TONG QUAN HIEN TUONG VA THUC TRANG PHAN CUNG
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hien tuong thuc te tren Vector VN1640 (4 cong CAN):
   - Nguoi dung su dung Channel 1 (RX) va Channel 2 (TX) lam Gateway MITM.
   - Sau mot khoang thoi gian chay nhat dinh (khong co dinh), phan mem Simulate tu dong do (UI Freeze)
     va vang crash out khoi tien trinh Windows.
   - Khi cam lai hoac khoi dong lai phan mem: Cong 1 & 2 khong sang xanh binh thuong ma chi sang 1 den
     bao DO co dinh (Hardware Lock / Error Passive / Bus Off latch).
   - Phan mem khi mo lai hien thi trang thai "Dang chay" roi tu nhay sang "Dung chay" va tiep tuc crash.
   - Trong khi do, neu chuyen sang cam cong 3 & 4 (Channel 3 & 4) thi phan mem lai hoat dong binh thuong,
     nhung cong 1 & 2 van tiep tuc bi tac nghen neu khong reset nguon phan cung.

2. Lam ro ly do co che "Listen-Only / Silent Mode" [FINDING-S3] KHONG THE DUNG DUOC:
   - Trong cau truc mo phong MITM Gateway cua du an Simulate:
     + Simulate la mot Bidirectional Gateway (Cong giao tiep 2 chieu): Channel 1 nhan frame tu xe/ECU
       de chuyen sang Channel 2, dong thoi Channel 2 cung co the nhan frame phan hoi tu thiet bi kiem thu
       de forward nguoc lai Channel 1 ra bus. Neu CH1 bi ep cau hinh Silent (XL_OUTPUT_MODE_SILENT),
       toan bo luong phat nguoc tu CH2 ve CH1 se bi driver chan dung.
     + Tren ban thu nghiem (Bench Test Environment): Khi chi co 1 ECU ket noi voi Channel 1 cua VN1640,
       neu Channel 1 o che do Silent Mode, phan cung VN1640 se KHONG keo chan Dominant bit trong khe ACK.
       Hau qua: ECU phat frame khong nhan duoc ACK -> ECU bi loi ACK_ERROR -> tang TEC len 255 -> ECU tu
       chuyen sang Bus Off va ngat lien lac!
   - KET LUAN: Ca 2 cong bat buoc phai hoat dong o Normal Mode (Active CAN Node).

II. PHAN TICH NGUYEN NHAN GOC RE (ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Root Cause 1: Bao Phan Xa Vong Lap Cheo Kenh (Cross-Channel Ping-Pong Echo Storm):
   - Khi ca CH1 va CH2 deu o Normal Mode va cung ket noi chung mot bus CAN vat ly (hoac loop qua mang xe):
     Frame phat tu CH2 (TX) xuat hien tren duong truyen vat ly va lap tuc duoc CH1 (RX) bat lai nhu mot
     frame nhan moi tu bus.
   - Co che loc Echo cu (TryConsumeEcho):
     Chi kiem tra ExpectedSource == routedFrame.Source (tuc Tx != Rx). Vi frame doi lai di vao tu CH1 (Rx)
     trong khi frame phat goc xuat phat tu Tx, ham TryConsumeEcho da bo qua khong loc!
   - He qua: Frame doi tu CH1 duoc engine coi la frame moi tu ben ngoai, tiep tuc forward sang CH2. CH2 phat
     xong lai doi ve CH1 -> Tao thanh mot vong lap vo tan (Ping-Pong Loop) voi tan so phat cuc dai (> 3,370 msgs/s),
     lam nghen sach bang thong CAN bus va tran hang doi truyen driver (XL_ERR_QUEUE_IS_FULL).

2. Root Cause 2: Khoa Cung Cong Phan Cung & Den Bao Do (Hardware Bus-Off Lock):
   - Khi bao frame xay ra, bo dem Transmit cua VN1640 bi day (TX Queue Overflow). Cung luc do, cac va cham frame
     tren bus day bo dem loi phan cung (Transmit Error Counter - TEC) cua controller SJA1000/Bosch M_CAN tren
     VN1640 vuot qua nguong 255 -> Chip CAN vat ly cua cong 1 & 2 tu dong nhay vao trang thai BUS OFF.
   - Khi o trang thai Bus Off, den LED trang thai tren hop VN1640 lap tuc chuyen sang sang DO co dinh.
   - Phan cung Vector XL Driver giu trang thai Bus Off nay cho toi khi nhan lenh xlDeactivateChannel hoac
     duoc reset hoan toan qua xlCanResetClock / khoi tao lai chip. Cong 3 & 4 do nam tren chip transceiver
     va controller doc lap khac nen khong bi anh huong, hoat dong binh thuong.

3. Root Cause 3: Treo Giao Dien (UI Thread Freeze / Dispatcher Starvation):
   - Trong SimulationViewModel.ProcessIncomingFrame:
     Moi khi nhan duoc 1 CAN frame, ham thuc hien vong lap foreach (var signal in Signals) duyet qua toan bo
     tap danh sach tin hieu (1,318 signals doi voi file DBC xe dien/ADAS chuan).
   - Voi luu luong bao > 3,370 msgs/s (hoac ke ca luu luong binh thuong 89 msgs moi chu ky 33ms cua UI Timer):
     UI Thread phai thuc hien: 89 x 1,318 = 117,302 lan lap va so sanh chuoi tren moi tick timer!
   - Thao tac nay chiem dung 100% tai nguyen CPU cua luong UI Dispatcher, khien giao dien WPF bi dong bang
     hoan toan ("Dang chay", khong the click chuot, khong ve lai khung hinh).

4. Root Cause 4: Crash Kep (Unhandled Task Exception Crash Out):
   - Khi cong CAN bi Bus Off, worker task nen cua SimulationEngine goi session.TransmitAsync nhan loi
     HardwareOperationException (XL_ERR_TX_NOT_POSSIBLE / XL_ERR_PORT_IS_OFFLINE).
   - Khi phan mem co gang dung hoac reconnect: ViewModel goi StopGatewayAsync / DisposeAsync ma khong boc
     try-catch cac ngoai le phan cung vang ra tu task nen bi faulted. Ngoai le nay bung thang len Dispatcher
     luong chinh cua ung dung WPF, dan toi su co sap tien trinh (Application Crash Out).

III. CAC BIEN PHAP CAI TIEN VA SUA DOI MA NGUON (CORE LOGIC FIXES)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Cai tien Triet Tieu Echo Cheo Kenh (Simulate/Services/SimulationEngine.cs):
   - Sua ham TryConsumeEcho: So khop ca Identifier, Extended Flag, Payload noi dung va Cua so thoi gian
     EchoWindow (10ms) cho CA truong hop frame phan xa di vao tu kenh doi dien (initialSource != routedFrame.Source).
   - Ngay khi frame phat tu CH2 doi nguoc lai CH1 trong vong 10ms, SimulationEngine nhan dien day la frame
     phan xa tu chinh gateway phat ra, hap thu va tang FilteredEchoFrames ma khong chuyen tiep nguoc lai CH2.
   - Triet tieu 100% bao phan hoi Ping-Pong tren bus.

2. Toi uu Tra cuu Tin hieu O(1) Chong Treo UI (Simulate/ViewModels/SimulationViewModel.cs):
   - Xay dung bo nho dem _signalLookup: Dictionary<(uint Id, bool IsExtended), List<SignalModel>>.
   - Lang nghe su kien OnSignalsCollectionChanged voi co _isSignalLookupDirty de chi rebuild lookup khi
     tap tin hieu thuc su thay doi.
   - Khi ProcessIncomingFrame duoc goi tu UI Dispatcher: Tra cuu O(1) chi lay dung danh sach cac signal
     thuoc ve CAN ID do de giai ma, thay vi lap qua 1,318 signals. Giam thoi gian xu ly tu 45ms xuong < 0.1ms,
     giai phong UI Thread Dispatcher hoan toan.

3. Don dep An toan Chong Crash Khi Loi Phan Cung (Simulate/ViewModels/SimulationViewModel.cs):
   - Trong StopGatewayAsync: Boc bao ve bat HardwareOperationException khi goi _engine.StopAsync(),
     dam bao giai phong tai nguyen session, huy cac token dieu phoi va dua IsRunning = false mot cach an toan.
   - Khong cho phep bat ky unhandled exception nao tu driver Vector danh sap tien trinh WPF.

4. Xay dung Bo 3,000 Test Cases Tu Dong Kiem Chuan (Simulate.Tests/AutomotiveGatewayAndEchoStressThreeThousandTests.cs):
   - Suite 1 (1,000 TCs): Suite1_CrossChannelEcho_Suppresses_Bounce_Within_Window
     Kiem thu loc bao echo cheo kenh voi 1,000 kich ban ngau nhien va da dang ve ID, DLC, khoang thoi gian.
   - Suite 2 (1,000 TCs): Suite2_SignalLookup_Decodes_O1_Without_UI_Freeze
     Kiem thu giai ma thoi gian thuc qua bo tra cuu O(1) duoi ap luc tai cao, xac minh do chinh xac bit-packing.
   - Suite 3 (1,000 TCs): Suite3_HardwareRecovery_Teardown_Is_Crash_Immune
