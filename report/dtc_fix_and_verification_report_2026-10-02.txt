   - Sua Padding Frame: Doi break; thanh continue; khi gap cum byte 00 00 00 00 hoac AA AA AA AA.
   - Sua dinh dang Dtc3ToJ2012: Them dau gach ngang phan tach phan nhom chuan SAE J2012 (${letter}{d1}{d2:X1}{b:X2}-{c:X2}).

   - Sua GetDtcEvaluation: Bo sung kiem tra bit 2 (pendingDTC), phan loai chuan xac 4 trang thai: Active (bit0 & bit3), Stored (bit3), Pending (bit0 hoac bit2), No Fault.
